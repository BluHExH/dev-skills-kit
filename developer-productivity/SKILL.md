# Developer Productivity

## Daily Task Management

Recommended simple system:
- Capture everything in one place (todo list or notes).
- Each day pick 1-3 Most Important Tasks (MITs).
- Time-block deep work sessions (60-90 minutes) without interruptions.
- Use a "waiting for" or "follow-up" list for things blocked on others.
- Review at the end of the day: what moved forward, what is left.

Avoid over-planning. A short daily list beats a complex system that is not maintained.

## Focus & Deep Work

- Turn off non-essential notifications during deep work.
- Use the Pomodoro technique or fixed time blocks.
- Batch shallow work (emails, messages, admin) into specific times.
- Protect mornings for the hardest cognitive tasks when possible.

## Learning New Technology Efficiently

1. Start with the official documentation and a small real project.
2. Build something useful instead of only following tutorials.
3. Focus on fundamentals first, then framework-specific patterns.
4. Teach or write about what you learn (forces clarity).
5. Limit parallel learning topics. Depth beats shallow breadth.

## Portfolio & Case Studies

A strong case study includes:
- The problem / context
- Your role and constraints
- Process and key decisions
- Challenges and how you solved them
- Final result + measurable impact if available
- What you would improve next time

Prefer quality over quantity. 3-5 strong projects are better than 15 weak ones.

## Sustainable Pace

- Avoid chronic overtime. Burnout destroys long-term output.
- Schedule recovery time.
- Track energy levels, not just hours worked.
- Continuously refine tools and processes that save mental effort.
